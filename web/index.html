Questions

Good to know

Is it really private?

Yes. When you run a model in Oriel, it runs inside your browser, on your own device. Your messages are never sent to a server — not ours, not anyone's — and your chats are saved only in your browser.

Is it really free?

Yes. Oriel costs nothing and needs no account, and every model here is free to download. A few models' licences only allow personal use; those are clearly marked.

Do I need the internet?

To open Oriel and to download a model the first time, yes. The model then runs on your device — your conversations never go online.

Why is the first time slow?

The model has to download once — from about 0.2 GB for the smallest up to 5 GB. After that it's stored in your browser and starts in seconds.

Which browsers work?

Running models in the browser needs WebGPU: recent Chrome or Edge on Windows, Mac, Linux or Chromebook, and Safari 26 or newer on a Mac. Phones can run the tiny models. Downloading a model to run with Ollama or LM Studio works from any browser.

Which model should I pick?

Start with Qwen3 4B if your computer has 8 GB of memory or more, or Llama 3.2 1B on an older one. For programming, try Qwen2.5 Coder 7B. To run on your computer with Ollama, Gemma 4 E4B is a great all-rounder that also reads photos.

Can I use these models for my business?

Most, yes — every model shows its licence in plain words, and you can filter for “Free for business use”.

How do I free up space?

On the Browse page, press Manage in the “Your device” box to delete models downloaded in this browser.
