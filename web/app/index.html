Questions

Good to know

Is it really private?

Yes. The AI itself — the model — downloads into your browser and runs on your own computer's graphics chip. What you type, the files you add and the pictures you make are never sent to a server: not ours, not anyone's. The only things Oriel downloads are the AI models, from Hugging Face where their makers publish them, and the open-source code that runs them, from the jsDelivr network — never anything you type or make.

Is it really free? What's the catch?

There's no catch. Because the AI runs on your computer, it costs us nothing when you use it — so there's nothing to charge for, and no reason to collect your data.

How good is it?

Very good for everyday things: explaining, writing, brainstorming, homework help, code and pictures. It isn't as clever as the biggest cloud AIs on hard questions, and it doesn't know today's news. Like every AI, it can make mistakes — check anything important.

Why does it download so much the first time?

That's the AI itself: about 2 GB for chatting, plus 6.6 GB the first time you ask for a picture (it asks first). It's saved on your computer, so it only downloads once — and if the download is interrupted, it carries on where it stopped.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once Oriel has downloaded its AI, it works with no internet at all — turn off your Wi-Fi and it keeps going. You can also install it from your browser's menu, to open it like any other app.

How long does a picture take?

About a minute on a MacBook Air — the whole picture is painted by your own computer's graphics chip. Faster computers are quicker. Chat answers start in about a second.

Which AI models does it use?

Open models from leading labs: a Qwen model from Alibaba for chat and code, and Z-Image Turbo from Alibaba's Tongyi lab for pictures. Oriel picks the best one your computer can run; you can choose another in the menu.

How do I delete everything?

Delete chats in Oriel's sidebar, and remove downloaded models in Settings. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes everything at once.
