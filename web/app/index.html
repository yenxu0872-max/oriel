Your questions. Your ideas. Your secrets. They stay yours.

Fair questions.

Is it really private?

Yes. Oriel’s AI runs inside your browser, on your own computer, and your chats are saved there too. Nothing you type is sent anywhere — once a model has downloaded, you can turn off the Wi-Fi and keep going.

How can it be free?

Your computer does the work, so there are no servers to pay for. No account, no subscription, no ads.

Does Oriel show ads or use my chats?

No. There are no ads, no accounts and no trackers. Your chats are saved in your browser, on your computer — Oriel has no server for them to go to.

Is it as smart as ChatGPT?

Not at everything. Cloud AI runs giant models in data centres; Oriel runs smaller ones that fit on your computer. They’re very good at everyday questions, writing, code and pictures — and they keep your secrets.

What do I need?

A laptop or desktop from the last few years, with Chrome, Edge or Safari 26. The recommended model is a 2.3 GB download; pictures add 6.6 GB. More memory lets you run bigger models.

How do I delete things?

Delete a chat from its menu and its pictures go with it. Models and the picture maker can be removed in Settings. It’s all on your computer, so deleting really deletes.

Where do the models come from?

They’re open models from Alibaba, Google, Meta, Microsoft, Mistral and others, downloaded once from Hugging Face. See them all on Oriel Models.

Who makes Oriel?

An independent project — not affiliated with Google, OpenAI or any model maker. The code is public on GitHub, so anyone can check what it does.
